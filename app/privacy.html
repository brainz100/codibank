제2조 개인정보의 이용
  • AI 기반 맞춤 코디 추천 및 착장 이미지·영상 생성
  • 회원 가입·인증·계정 관리 및 동의 기록 관리
  • 유료 플랜 결제 처리 및 이용 내역 관리
  • 서비스 이용 통계 분석 및 품질 개선 (선택 동의 시 비식별 처리한 생성 이미지·신체데이터 포함)
  • 부정 이용 탐지 및 서비스 보안 유지
  • 법령상 의무 이행

수집된 개인정보는 위 목적 이외의 용도로 사용되지 않으며, 제3자에게 판매하거나 광고 목적으로 제공하지 않습니다.

제3조 보유·이용 기간 및 파기

회사는 개인정보의 처리 목적이 달성되면 지체 없이 파기합니다. 전자파일은 복구할 수 없는 방법으로 영구 삭제하고, 출력물은 분쇄 또는 소각합니다.

회원 탈퇴 시 계정, 프로필, 신체데이터(얼굴 사진 포함), 옷장·앨범 데이터, 옷장코디 예약 정보, 사용 기록은 지체 없이 파기합니다. AI가 생성한 이미지 파일은 계정과 연결되지 않은 임의 주소로 저장되어 본인 외에는 접근할 수 없으며, 이용약관 제9조의 보관 기간에 따라 삭제됩니다.

다만 다음 정보는 다른 개인정보와 분리하여 아래 기간 동안 보관한 후 파기합니다.

제4조 개인정보의 제3자 제공

회사는 이용자의 개인정보를 제3자에게 제공하지 않습니다. 다만 이용자가 사전에 동의한 경우 또는 법령에 근거가 있는 경우(수사기관의 적법한 요청 등)는 예외로 합니다.

Article 1. Personal Information Collected and Purposes

Sign-up (required): email, password (hashed), consent record — member identification. Kept until account deletion.

Profile: (required) gender, age group; (optional) nickname, phone number — personalized recommendations. Kept until account deletion.

Body data (separate consent): face and full-body photos, height, weight, body type, body measurements, personal color results — outfit image generation, body type analysis, recommendations. Destroyed without delay upon deletion request or account deletion. Face photos are used only for image generation; no biometric templates for identification are created.

Closet & generated data: clothing photos and analysis, AI-generated images, outfit album. Kept until account deletion.

Location: current location (GPS or IP based) — weather/season-based recommendations. Kept until account deletion.

Usage records: usage counts, access logs, device info, IP — operations and fraud prevention. 1 year.

Payments: payment method (processed by PG), payment history — 5 years (E-Commerce Act).

Article 2. Use

AI outfit recommendations and image/video generation; account and consent management; payments; statistics and quality improvement (de-identified images/body data only with optional consent); fraud prevention; legal compliance. Personal information is never sold or provided for advertising.

Article 3. Retention and Destruction

Upon account deletion, the account, profile, body data (including face photos), closet/album data, scheduled outfit data and usage records are destroyed without delay. AI-generated image files are stored at random, unguessable addresses and deleted according to Article 9 of the Terms.

Kept separately: a one-way hash of the deleted member's email with that month's usage and deletion reasons (fraud prevention, 1 year); consent records with a pseudonymized email (3 years from consent); pseudonymized generation statistics; payment/contract records (5 years), complaint records (3 years), advertising records (6 months), access logs (3 months).

Article 4. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties except with prior consent or as required by law.

Article 5. Processing Delegation and Overseas Transfer

Data is transferred encrypted (HTTPS) at the time of use to: Google LLC (USA, AI analysis/image generation), OpenAI (USA, some AI analysis/generation), BytePlus Pte. Ltd. (Singapore, outfit video generation), Cloudflare, Inc. (USA, file storage), Supabase, Inc. (USA, authentication/database), Render Services, Inc. (USA, servers in Singapore), Vercel Inc. (USA, web hosting). Domestic: Korea PortOne and KG Inicis (payments). You may refuse the overseas transfer, but core features cannot be provided without it.

Article 6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing or withdrawal of consent at any time: delete your face photo/body info in Profile, change optional consent in My Page → Manage consent, delete your account in My Page, or contact the Privacy Officer (processed within 10 days).

Article 7. Local Storage and Analytics

We use browser localStorage/sessionStorage for login and settings, and Google Analytics for usage statistics. You can clear or block these in your browser settings.

Article 8. Security Measures

HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords, server-only database admin keys with least privilege, hash-only storage for deleted members, minimal staff access and access logging.
